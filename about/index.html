About the Founder

Professional background, certifications, and the mission behind Carlson Solutions LLC.

Caleb Carlson

Caleb Carlson is the Founder and Owner of Carlson Solutions LLC in Chisago County, Minnesota. With an Electrical Engineering background and high-level experience developed through both formal academic training and hands-on field delivery, he launched the company in 2025 to provide dependable, tailored technology solutions for homeowners, nonprofits, and small businesses.

The company began with IT and network-focused services, including computer troubleshooting, firewall and Wi-Fi setup, and infrastructure configuration. As client demand evolved, Carlson Solutions LLC expanded in 2026 to include energy auditing services, broadening its mission to combine technical reliability with measurable operational efficiency.

Today, the service model aligns with the company's core offerings: IT Management, Security Consulting, Energy Auditing, Office Tech Setup, UniFi Protect Setup, and Custom Project Requests.
